ABOUT PRINCE LUBRICANTS

What is PRINCE LUBRICANTS?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS is a Singapore-based high-performance lubricant manufacturer specialising in highly-advanced automotive, marine and industrial lubrication solutions. Its portfolio covers passenger cars, motorcycles, commercial vehicles, motorsport, transmissions and drivetrains, as well as marine equipment and industrial machinery, with products distributed across more than 30 international markets.

Who manufactures PRINCE LUBRICANTS?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS products are manufactured by PRINCE GLOBAL PTE. LTD. in Singapore. PRINCE GLOBAL PTE. LTD. supports lubricant manufacturing, base-oil operations and international supply across the PRINCE LUBRICANTS portfolio.

Where is PRINCE LUBRICANTS based?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS is based in Singapore, where its lubricant manufacturing and international operations are supported by PRINCE GLOBAL PTE. LTD. Singapore serves as a base for the brand’s production, product development and supply to overseas markets.

02 Ester & Synthetic Technology

OUR TECHNOLOGY

19 questions

Why does PRINCE LUBRICANTS use ester technology?

Ester molecules have natural polarity, giving them a strong affinity for metal surfaces. This helps maintain a protective lubricating layer on critical engine components while supporting reduced friction, wear protection and high-temperature performance.

Which PRINCE LUBRICANTS products use P-9 ESTER Based Technology?

P-9 ESTER Based Technology is featured in flagship high-performance PRINCE LUBRICANTS products, including the FS1 and FS1 EUROGEN passenger car engine oil range and FSR motorsport racing lubricant range.

What is P-9 ESTER Based Technology?

P-9 ESTER Based Technology is a proprietary esterification technology engineered by PRINCE LUBRICANTS for high-performance engine oils. It incorporates polar ester chemistry to enhance lubrication, oil film retention, thermal stability, viscosity retention and improves overall protection across a wide operating temperature range.

Why are esters used in high-performance engine oils?

Esters combine strong polarity with excellent lubricity and thermal characteristics, making them valuable components in high-performance lubricant formulations. Their affinity for metal surfaces can enhance lubrication and protection under demanding operating conditions.

What does polar molecular protection mean?

Polar ester molecules are naturally attracted to metallic surfaces, allowing the lubricant to adhere more effectively to engine components. This provides an additional lubrication mechanism that helps protect critical moving surfaces during demanding operating conditions.

How does P-9 ESTER Based Technology perform at high temperatures?

P-9 ESTER Based Technology is designed to maintain lubricant stability and protective performance under elevated engine temperatures. Its ester formulation contributes to strong thermal stability, lubricity and resistance to lubricant heat-related degradation.

How does P-9 ESTER Based Technology improve oil film strength?

P-9 ESTER Based Technology enhances lubricant film integrity under high temperatures and mechanical loads. Its polar characteristics help the lubricant remain associated with metal surfaces where protection is most critical.

How does P-9 ESTER Based Technology help during cold starts?

P-9 ESTER Based Technology combines strong surface affinity with excellent low-temperature lubricant characteristics, helping critical components remain protected as lubrication begins circulating after start-up.

How does ester technology contribute to engine cleanliness?

High-quality ester components can contribute to formulation solvency and optimized deposit control. Combined with a specialized detergent-dispersant agents, P-9 ESTER Based Technology helps maintain cleaner internal engine surfaces and components and prevent the buildup of harmful deposits.

What is the difference between P-9 ESTER and P-10 ESTER Based Technology?

Both are proprietary ester technologies developed by PRINCE LUBRICANTS, but they are designed around different lubrication environments. P-9 focuses on high-performance automotive lubrication, while P-10 is specifically formulated around the combined engine, transmission and clutch demands of motorcycles.

Why was P-10 ESTER Based Technology developed specifically for motorcycles?

Motorcycle lubricants can simultaneously lubricate the engine, gearbox and wet clutch, exposing the oil to intense thermal and mechanical stresses. P-10 ESTER Based Technology is designed around these specialised lubrication requirements.

What is P-10 ESTER Based Technology?

P-10 ESTER Based Technology is a proprietary PRINCE LUBRICANTS ester technology developed specifically for high-performance motorcycle lubrication. It is formulated to withstand the high RPMS, high engine speeds, loads, temperatures and combined engine-transmission stresses found in powerful motorcycles.

How does P-10 ESTER Based Technology protect motorcycle engines and transmissions?

P-10 ESTER Based Technology provides strong shear and lubrication stability under high RPM, elevated temperatures and gearbox shear. Its ester chemistry supports wear protection and lubricant performance across critical motorcycle components.

Does P-10 ESTER Based Technology support wet-clutch performance?

P-10 ESTER Based Technology is incorporated into motorcycle lubricant formulations designed to provide the controlled friction characteristics required by wet-clutch systems while maintaining dependable engine and gearbox protection.

What is P+ SYNTHESE Technology?

P+ SYNTHESE is a PRINCE LUBRICANTS formulation technology incorporating a high proportion of severely hydroisomerized, high-viscosity-index base stocks. It provides enhanced oxidation stability, viscosity performance and protection compared with conventional mineral-based formulations.

How is P+ SYNTHESE different from conventional semi-synthetic technology?

P+ SYNTHESE places greater emphasis on high-quality, severely hydroisomerized high VI base stocks rather than relying on a simple mineral-and-synthetic blend description. The technology is designed to deliver stronger thermal stability, oxidation resistance, cleanliness and lasting-lubricant performance.

Does a higher ester content automatically mean a better engine oil?

Not necessarily. Overall lubricant performance depends on the complete formulation, including base oil chemistry, ester selection, viscosity characteristics, additive technology and compatibility between individual components.

Does a higher synthetic content always mean better lubricant performance?

Not by itself. A lubricant must be formulated for its intended engine, operating conditions and performance specifications; base oil quality and additive chemistry must work together as a complete system.

Why is base oil quality important in lubricant formulation?

Base oils influence viscosity stability, volatility, oxidation resistance, low-temperature performance and lubricant durability. Selecting the appropriate base system is therefore fundamental to the performance of the finished lubricant.

Yes. PRINCE LUBRICANTS FSR is particularly relevant to modified and high-output engines operating beyond conventional production-engine conditions. Racing lubrication requires more than a single performance characteristic. The combination of P-9 and P-12 ESTER Based Chemistry enables FSR to optimise polar surface protection, lubricity, oil film integrity and high-temperature performance as part of a complete racing lubricant system.

How does PRINCE FSR maintain oil film strength at high temperatures?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS FSR combines thermally stable PAO base stocks with high-polarity P-9 and P-12 ESTER Based Chemistry to maintain robust lubricant film characteristics as temperature and load increase. This is critical for protecting highly loaded bearings, valvetrain components, pistons and other racing engine surfaces.

How does PRINCE FSR protect an engine at sustained high RPM?

At sustained high RPM, the lubricant is subjected to intense shear, aeration, heat and rapidly changing loads. PRINCE LUBRICANTS FSR is formulated to maintain viscosity stability, oil film integrity and unsurpassed anti-wear protection under these severe dynamic conditions.

How does PRINCE FSR protect highly loaded engine bearings?

Racing bearings experience extremely high loads and oil film stress. PRINCE LUBRICANTS FSR combines strong and robust high-temperature film characteristics, polar ester chemistry, low-volatility behaviour, and specialised anti-wear technology to maintain separation and protection between heavily loaded moving surfaces.

How important is shear stability in PRINCE FSR?

Shear stability is critical in extreme racing because the lubricant experiences intense mechanical stress at severely high engine speeds and loads. PRINCE LUBRICANTS FSR is formulated to resist excessive viscosity loss and maintain protective layer characteristics throughout severe competition use.

How does PRINCE FSR control oxidation during racing?

Extreme engine temperatures accelerate lubricant oxidation. PRINCE LUBRICANTS FSR combines oxidation-resistant synthetic and optimized ester components with a robust antioxidant system to limit lubricant degradation, viscosity change and deposit formation during severe high-temperature operation.

How does PRINCE FSR control deposits in racing engines?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS FSR combines thermally stable base oil chemistry with advanced detergent-dispersant and antioxidant systems to control oxidation products, varnish and high-temperature deposits. This helps maintain cleanliness in critical areas exposed to intense combustion and thermal loading.

Does a thicker FSR automatically provide greater protection?

No. Effective racing lubrication depends on maintaining the correct balance between oil flow and film thickness. Excessive viscosity can compromise circulation and efficiency, while insufficient viscosity may reduce oil-film strength under severe load.

Is PRINCE FSR simply a higher-viscosity version of PRINCE FS1?

No. PRINCE LUBRICANTS FSR is a dedicated racing lubricant platform with its own formulation philosophy. While both ranges draw on PRINCE LUBRICANTS ester expertise, FSR incorporates P-9 and P-12 ESTER Based Chemistry and is specifically formulated additive system around the thermal, mechanical and lubrication demands of severe competition environments.

What is the PRINCE MAXX motorcycle lubricant range?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS MAXX is the high-performance motorcycle engine oil family from PRINCE LUBRICANTS. The range includes MAXX GOLD, the signature ultra-high-performance fully synthetic P-10 ESTER series, and MAXX ULTRA, an advanced synthetic range featuring leading-edge P+ SYNTHESE Technology.

What is the difference between MAXX GOLD and MAXX ULTRA?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS MAXX GOLD represents the top-of-the-range, highest-performance motorcycle lubrication platform from PRINCE LUBRICANTS, combining fully synthetic base oils with proprietary P-10 ESTER Based Technology. MAXX ULTRA uses P+ SYNTHESE Technology to provide dependable synthetic performance, protection and durability across a broad range of motorcycle applications.

Why are motorcycle engine oils different from passenger car engine oils?

Many motorcycles use a common lubricant for the engine, transmission and wet clutch. The oil must therefore withstand high engine speeds, gearbox shear and elevated temperatures while maintaining the controlled friction characteristics required for reliable clutch operation.

What is PRINCE MAXX GOLD?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS MAXX GOLD is the top-of-the-line ester-based fully synthetic motorcycle engine oil series from PRINCE LUBRICANTS. It incorporates proprietary P-10 ESTER Based Technology for exceptional lubrication, viscosity stay-in-grade, oil film strength and protection under high-RPM, high-temperature and high-load motorcycle operation.

Is PRINCE MAXX GOLD suitable for high-performance motorcycles?

Yes. PRINCE LUBRICANTS MAXX GOLD is specifically engineered for high-performance motorcycle applications where high engine speeds, elevated temperatures and substantial mechanical loads place exceptional demands on the lubricant.

How does PRINCE MAXX GOLD protect high-revving motorcycle engines?

At sustained high RPM, motorcycle oil experiences intense heat, shear and rapidly changing mechanical loads. PRINCE LUBRICANTS MAXX GOLD is formulated to preserve viscosity stability, lubricant-film integrity and anti-wear protection under these demanding conditions.

Does ester technology affect wet-clutch performance?

Ester chemistry must be carefully balanced within the complete motorcycle lubricant formulation. In PRINCE LUBRICANTS MAXX GOLD, P-10 ESTER Based Technology is integrated with application-specific friction chemistry to provide ultimate-performance lubrication without compromising the required wet-clutch characteristics.

Is PRINCE MAXX GOLD suitable for everyday road motorcycles?

Yes. Its high-performance formulation is not limited to extreme environment use. PRINCE LUBRICANTS MAXX GOLD can provide premium protection for everyday motorcycles where its viscosity grade and performance specifications correspond with the manufacturer's requirements.

How does MAXX GOLD protect under extreme gearbox shear?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS MAXX GOLD combines shear-stable viscosity characteristics with the strong surface affinity and oil film performance of P-10 ESTER Based Technology. This helps preserve lubrication across highly loaded gears while maintaining protection throughout the shared engine lubrication circuit.

What is P+ SYNTHESE Technology in MAXX ULTRA?

P+ SYNTHESE Technology incorporates a high proportion of severely hydroisomerized, high VI base stocks within a state-of-the-art formulation system. This provides enhanced thermal stability, oxidation resistance and viscosity performance compared with conventional based motorcycle oils.

Who should choose MAXX GOLD instead of MAXX ULTRA?

PRINCE LUBRICANTS MAXX GOLD is positioned for riders seeking the highest-performance motorcycle lubricant technology within the PRINCE LUBRICANTS range, particularly for high engine capacity, high-output and demanding applications. MAXX ULTRA provides advanced synthetic performance for riders requiring strong protection and optimal durability across broader everyday motorcycle use.
