SUPER-SHIFT

SUPER-SHIFT GL-4/GL-5 75W-90

Gear teeth don’t just mesh — they clash, shear, and work under brutal force. Our high-performance formula tackles the pressure head-on, delivering extreme-pressure protection, friction optimization, noise suppression, and shear resistance for all driveline applications — including the latest-generation of synchronized and unsynchronized systems, hypoid gears, differentials, axles, and final drive systems.

  • 1 Liter
SpecificationAPI GL-4 · API GL-5 · MT-1
Product Enquiry ›Become a Distributor

Application

Developed for the synchronized and non- synchronized manual transmissions, axle gears and differentials in passenger cars, vans, buses, trucks and off-highway equipment requiring a gear oil with this specification.

Performance Levels
