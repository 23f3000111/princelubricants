PRINCE® PRO-SHIFT GT RACING GL-5 SAE 75W-140

Developed to withstand extreme torque loads and severe thermal stress in competitive motorsport, PRINCE® PRO-SHIFT GT RACING GL-5 SAE 75W-140 is an advanced fully synthetic racing gear oil reinforced with a cutting-edge extreme-pressure (EP) additive package and specialized limited-slip friction modifiers. Its robust SAE 75W-140 viscosity profile delivers exceptional lubricating film strength under aggressive torque transfer, repeated shock loads and prolonged high-temperature operation, safeguarding heavily loaded hypoid gears and bearings against scuffing, pitting and adhesive wear. Its advanced shear-stable formulation is engineered to maintain viscosity integrity, controlled limited-slip differential engagement and consistent drivetrain protection under the most punishing competitive conditions.

Intended Applications

Specifically developed for ultra-high-torque and severely loaded drivetrain assemblies in extensively modified, race-ready and high-performance sports vehicles, including heavy-duty hypoid drive axles, conventional and limited-slip differentials (LSD), non-synchronised racing gearboxes and transaxles requiring SAE 75W-140 gear oil meeting GL-5 performance requirements.
