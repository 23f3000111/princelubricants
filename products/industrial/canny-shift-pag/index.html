Gear Oil

CANNY-SHIFT PAG

Available in ISO Grade 150, 220, 320, and 460.

A specially developed poly-alkylene-glycol (PAG) based industrial gear oil with high viscosity index (VI) and very low pour point characteristics for gear applications that required low coefficient of friction. Features elevated anti-oxidant properties, contains extreme pressure (EP) additive package to ensure high thermal stability and excellent load carrying performance.

Available sizes
  • 20 Liter
  • 200 Liter
Product Enquiry ›Become a Distributor

Application

Designed especially for applications in worm reduction gear boxes, where the low coefficient of friction of the PAG base oil improves efficiency and consequently reduces power consumption and lower operating temperatures, which are particularly important in applications where sliding contact is very high.

Application of this range of product when the manufacturer prescribed/ allowed a PAG based fluid.

The viscosity selection must be followed assembly or manufacturer’s recommendations Selections are mainly based on the operating temperature, operating speed, load condition and pressure applied through the industrial gear system.

Not miscible with mineral oils.

Benefits

  • PAG base oils for wide temperature applications
  • EP additive for minimum wear
  • Extend service life
  • Reduce overall energy consumption
  • Low coefficient of friction
  • Anti-rust and anti-corrosion
  • Excellent thermal stability
  • High load carrying ability
  • Outstanding water separation behaviour
  • Compatible with most seal materials
