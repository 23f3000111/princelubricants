Cylinder Oils

MARINO CYDERR HS-RF 70

A marine cylinder lubricant that has been developed for low-speed two-stroke engines using high sulfur residual fuels. Contains conventional calcium carbonate chemical compound that helps efficiently neutralize sulfuric acid formed during combustion of high sulfur residual fuels.

Available sizes
  • 200 Liter
SpecificationMAN ES (B&W) · Wärtsilä
Product Enquiry ›Become a Distributor

Application

Suitable for all low-speed 2-stroke engines running on high sulfur residual fuels. Can be used alternately with lower TBN oils when using IMO 2020 compliant fuels.

Compatible with generally and commercially available cylinder oils.

Benefits

  • Minimize deposits build-up
  • High acid neutralization capability
  • Prevent corrosion due to sulfuric acid formation
  • Reduce cylinder scuffing and wear
  • Reduce overall maintenance cost
