Racing Gear Oils

PRO-SHIFT GT Racing GL-5 75W-140

PAO and ester-based heavy-duty gear oil engineered specifically for the axle drives, differential gears and manual gearboxes running at maximum load during race require the viscosity grade of SAE 75W-140. Exceptional EP and load-bearing properties allow the unsurpassed levels of shock load, high speed and high torque protection.

Available sizes
  • 1 Liter
SpecificationAPI GL-5 · API GL-5 +LS · API MT-1

Application

This product ensures ultimate protection in the most extreme load manual transmissions, axle drives with multi-disc limited slip differentials in competition cars and trucks. Dosed with an appropriate amount of friction modifier agents for use in limited slip differential gears, thus no additional additive is recommended.
