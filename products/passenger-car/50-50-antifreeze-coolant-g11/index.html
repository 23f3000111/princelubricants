PRINCE® 50/50 ANTIFREEZE/COOLANT G11 −37°C

PRINCE® 50/50 ANTIFREEZE/COOLANT G11 −37°C is a premixed, ready-to-use, premium ethylene glycol-based coolant formulated with advanced silicated Hybrid Organic Acid Technology (HOAT) to deliver dependable anti-corrosion performance, efficient heat transfer, improved winter protection, and long-term cooling system durability. Its hybrid inhibitor chemistry provides effective protection against corrosion, cavitation and scale formation, safeguarding both aluminium and cast iron radiators, cylinder heads, water pumps and other vital radiator components. Premixed with demineralised water at a 50:50 ratio, it provides freeze protection down to −37°C, effective boil-over resistance and dependable cooling performance without further dilution.
