PRINCE® ATF LV MULTI-VEHICLE

Engineered to meet the evolving performance requirements of modern multi-speed automatic transmissions, PRINCE® ATF LV fully synthetic automatic transmission fluid combines advanced low-viscosity fluid technology with comprehensive protection for demanding transmission systems. Expertly formulated with severely hydroisomerized synthetic base stocks and a sophisticated additive system, it provides excellent low-temperature fluidity, efficient hydraulic pressure transmission, and reduced internal fluid resistance for optimized drivetrain operating efficiency. Its robust shear stability and detergent-dispersant chemistry help maintain consistent viscosity characteristics while minimizing harmful deposits and varnish within sensitive valve bodies and hydraulic control circuits. Carefully engineered frictional properties further support progressive clutch engagement, smooth power transfer, and reliable shift performance throughout varying driving conditions.

Intended Applications

Developed for a broad range of passenger cars, SUVs, and light-duty commercial vehicles equipped with compatible conventional 5-, 6-, 7-, and 8-speed automatic transmission configurations. Particularly suited for modern multi-vehicle applications requiring low-viscosity, energy-conserving transmission fluids with stable frictional performance and optimized hydraulic response.
