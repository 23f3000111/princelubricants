PRINCE® ATF SV MULTI-VEHICLE

PRINCE® ATF SV is a high-viscosity, multi-vehicle transmission fluid formulated for reliable performance and dependable protection across conventional automatic transmission systems. Designed with a carefully balanced additive technology, its standard-viscosity characteristics provide robust lubricating film strength, consistent hydraulic pressure control, and effective protection against friction-related wear and scuffing. Modern friction-modifying chemistry promotes smooth gear engagement and stable torque converter clutch operation, while excellent thermal and oxidation stability helps minimize fluid degradation and additive depletion under prolonged service interval conditions. Enhanced detergent-dispersant properties further control deposit and varnish formation, supporting cleaner valve bodies, reliable shift solenoid operation, and sustained transmission efficiency and performance.

Intended Applications

For a wide range of applications in passenger cars, SUVs, and commercial vehicles equipped with conventional 3-speed and 4-speed automatic transmissions, as well as selected modern 5-speed automatic transmissions requiring standard-viscosity; high-viscosity fluid properties. Suitable for compatible older-generation automatic transmission systems requiring stable frictional characteristics, outstanding hydraulic performance, and effective wear and sludge protection.
