PRINCE® FS SUPER SAE 0W-30 SP-RC

PRINCE® FS SUPER SAE 0W-30 SP-RC Fully Synthetic Motor Oil is formulated with carefully chosen, advanced synthetic base stocks and a balanced additive system to deliver dependable engine protection, improved energy and fuel efficiency, and consistent lubrication performance in gasoline engines equipped with or without a turbocharger. Developed to meet Resource Conserving performance requirements, its reduced-viscosity formulation helps reduce internal friction and energy losses while maintaining uncompromising wear protection across varying operating conditions. Refined anti-wear and other additive system provide safeguarding against low-speed pre-ignition (LSPI), timing chain wear, sludge formation, and high-temperature piston deposits. Excellent low-temperature fluidity promotes fast oil circulation during cold starts, while excellent thermal stability helps maintain lubricant performance at elevated operating temperatures. Ideally suited for modern naturally aspirated, turbocharged gasoline engines requiring SAE 0W-30 or SAE 5W-30 lubrication.
