PRINCE® FS1 TECHNICA SAE 0W-30 C2

PRINCE® FS1 TECHNICA SAE 0W-30 C2 Fully Synthetic Motor Oil integrates severely hydroisomerized Group III synthetic base stocks, proprietary Group V P-9 Ester Based Technology, and a precisely calibrated additive package to deliver optimized fuel economy, superior thermal-oxidative stability, and sustained deposit and wear protection. Formulated to meet ACEA C2 requirements with HTHS viscosity ≥2.9 mPa·s, its friction-optimized formulation minimizes hydrodynamic drag while maintaining lubricating film integrity, shear stability, and unparalleled anti-wear performance. Advanced detergent-dispersant chemistry promotes piston deposit control, sludge resistance, and lubricant cleanliness throughout the proposed service interval, while controlled phosphate ash and sulphur chemistry supports exhaust aftertreatment compatibility and operating efficiency. Purpose-developed for fuel-efficiency oriented passenger vehicle gasoline and diesel engines in selected BMW, Citroën, Peugeot, Fiat, and Range Rover.

Intended Applications

Meticulously formulated for modern fuel-efficient gasoline and diesel engines in passenger cars and SUVs, including turbocharged and direct-injection configurations (GDI/TGDI), requiring SAE 0W-30, ACEA C2 specified low-SAPS oils. Designed for selected BMW, PSA and Land Rover passenger vehicle models equipped with advanced exhaust aftertreatment systems, where fuel-economy oriented, low-HTHS lubricants are required. Also suitable for today’s Asian downsized gasoline and hybrid passenger car engines requiring SAE 0W-30 oils meeting API SP Resource Conserving (SP-RC) and/or ACEA A5/B5 performance requirements, subject to verified product compliance and vehicle manufacturer recommendations.
