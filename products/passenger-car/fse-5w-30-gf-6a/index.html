PRINCE® FSe SAE 5W-30 GF-6A

Combining precision-controlled viscosity with advanced friction modifiers to maximize engine operating efficiency, PRINCE® FSe SAE 5W-30 Fully Synthetic Motor Oil integrates severely hydroisomerized Group III synthetic base stocks, advanced friction-modifying chemistry, and proprietary Anti-Wear Booster Technology to deliver reduced mechanical energy losses and sustained engine efficiency and protection. Formulated to meet API SP Resource Conserving and ILSAC GF-6A performance requirements, its low-HTHS viscosity characteristics promote improved fuel economy while maintaining lubricating film integrity and superior anti-wear performance under varying driving conditions. The advanced additive technology provides exceptional resistance to low-speed pre-ignition (LSPI), enhanced timing chain and valvetrain wear protection, and superior control of piston deposits, varnish and sludge formation. Combining rapid low-temperature oil circulation with optimized high-temperature oxidation resistance and shear stability, the formulation maintains reliable lubricating behaviour throughout demanding thermal cycles.

Intended Applications

Specially developed for gasoline and hybrid powertrains in passenger cars and SUVs, including naturally aspirated, turbocharged, direct injection (GDI/TGDI), and battery-assisted configurations such as mild hybrid electric vehicles (MHEVs) and plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs). Suitable for engines equipped with or without gasoline particulate filters (GPF) and catalytic converters.
