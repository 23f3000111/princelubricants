POWER STEERING FLUID STOP LEAK

A very high quality PSF that was developed for use in a power steering system that specified ATF GM DEXRON® II and III fluids. Contains 'stop-leak additive' to minimizes leaks caused by worn or deteriorated seals.

Description

Produced with the combination of premium mineral base stock and stop-leak additive technology. This product helps renew, restore and condition shrunken and hardened seals in the system. It rejuvenates deteriorated or worn seals to minimize and prevent future leaks.

Special anti-shudder additives were added to elevate wear protection, reduce noise and chatter in the power steering pump as well as prolong system service life.

Application

PRINCE POWER STEERING FLUID WITH STOP LEAK features conventional formula designed for use in all manufacturers in the world. Applicable in passenger cars, SUVs and light trucks, high and low mileage.

Compatible with other power steering fluids in the market. Can be used for service refill and topping up existing systems.

If a leak continues, a second application may be required, mechanical attention is also needed (leaks usually stop in 3 days and around 300km of driving after using this product).

This product should not be used in Honda power steering systems and systems that use specific synthetic blend central system hydraulic fluids.

Benefits

  • Additives are added to stop power systems leaks
  • Assisting in reducing clogs, wear and ‘squeal’
  • Conventional formula for use in all manufacturers
  • Excellent foam control and resists oxidation
  • Protect against pump failure
  • Provide maximum year-around power steering system protection
  • Reduce corrosion and rust in the system
  • Reduce noise and chatter

This product is well-suited for application where vehicle manufacturers call for conventional ATF Fluids such as GM DEXRON®, DEXRON® II, DEXRON® III, Ford M2C138-CJ, Mercon.
