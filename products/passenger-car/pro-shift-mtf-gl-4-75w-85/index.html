PRINCE® PRO-SHIFT MTF GL-4 SAE 75W-85

Expertly developed to deliver uncompromising performance across modern manual transmission architectures, PRINCE® PRO-SHIFT MTF GL-4 SAE 75W-85 incorporates advanced fully synthetic base stocks and a specialized additive system tailored to the frictional requirements of a wide range of synchronized gearboxes. Its optimized SAE 75W-85 viscosity characteristics establish a resilient lubricating film across highly loaded gear contacts, providing effective resistance against surface fatigue, scuffing and premature wear. Specialized synchronizer-compatible friction chemistry supports controlled speed equalization during gear selection, promoting consistent engagement without compromising the functional characteristics of synchronizer materials. Excellent resistance to thermal degradation and mechanical shearing helps maintain lubricant integrity under sustained operating loads, while effective deposit and varnish control contribute to the long-term preservation of internal transmission assemblies.

Intended Applications

Developed for passenger cars, performance vehicles, SUVs, and light commercial vehicles incorporating synchronized manual transmissions and integrated transaxle assemblies. Intended for selected European, Japanese and Korean drivetrain applications requiring SAE 75W-85 API GL-4 lubrication in accordance with manufacturer-prescribed specifications.
