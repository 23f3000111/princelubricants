# PRINCE LUBRICANTS

> Singapore-based high-performance lubricant specialist, established in 1998, serving automotive, racing, two-wheeler, marine and industrial applications worldwide. Licensed by PRINCE OIL INC. U.S.A. and manufactured by PRINCE GLOBAL PTE. LTD.

PRINCE LUBRICANTS develops performance-driven and advanced lubricants for demanding engines, machinery and operating environments. The business began in 1998 in the sourcing, bulk trading and regional distribution of base stocks to lubricant manufacturers. It then expanded into toll blending, finished-lubricant manufacturing (including contract and private-label manufacturing), marine lubricant supply and international distribution, all consolidated under PRINCE GLOBAL PTE. LTD.

## Key facts

- More than 100,000 metric tonnes of annual blending capacity, and more than 15,000 MT of base oil storage capacity.
- More than 40,000 MT of annual base oil supply volume, and more than 40 factories served across Southeast Asia and South Asia.
- 70+ API-licensed products and 200+ racing partnerships.
- A presence in more than 30 countries through importers, exclusive distributors and market partners, including the United Kingdom, Australia, China, Saudi Arabia, India, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia, the Philippines, Papua New Guinea, Egypt, Ethiopia, Kenya, Oman, Bangladesh, the Maldives and Fiji.
- Quality marks: ISO 9001:2015, API ENERGY, API STARBURST, ACEA and EELQMS.

## Technologies and products

- P-9 ESTER Based Technology: the formulation architecture behind the FS1 Series, FS1 EUROGEN Series and FSR Racing Series, for modern automotive and racing performance.
- P-10 ESTER Based Technology: dedicated ester technology for powerful 4T motorcycle engines, used in the MAXX GOLD Series.
- P-12 Ester-Based Technology: used in the D1 GOLD and D1 GOLD 4X4 heavy-duty diesel engine oils. The FSR racing engine oils use Group V double ester (P-9/P-12) base stocks.
- P+ SYNTHESE: a high concentration of severely hydroisomerized, high-viscosity-index base stocks, used in SS1, D1 and MAXX ULTRA engine oils.
- High-performance formulations are built on high-purity hydroisomerized VHVI (Very-High-Viscosity Index) base stocks.
- Product categories: Passenger Car, Competition, Commercial Vehicle, Powersports, Industrial and Marine. All PRINCE LUBRICANTS products are manufactured by PRINCE GLOBAL PTE. LTD. in Singapore.
- Passenger Car: 64 products in eight types. Flagship ester based engine oils (FS1, FS1 EUROGEN, FS1 TECHNICA), passenger car engine oils (FSe, FS SUPER, SS1, SS SUPER, M1), automatic transmission fluids (ATF EUROSPEC, ATF, DCT, CVT), manual transmission oils (PRO-SHIFT MTF), differential and hypoid gear oils (SUPER-SHIFT), hydraulic and power steering fluids, brake and clutch fluids (DOT 5.1, DOT 4, DOT 3), and antifreeze coolants (G11, G12/G12+, G12++/G13, G12 EVO).
- Competition: 10 products in four types. FSR racing engine oils (FSR, FSR GT ENDURANCE, FSR GT EXTREME, SAE 5W-40 to 10W-60), PRO-SHIFT GT RACING gear oils (API GL-5, GL-5 +LS), BRAKE FLUID GT RACING (DOT 4) and GT COOLING glycol-free racing coolants.
- Commercial Vehicle: 44 products in eight types, 14 of them shared with Passenger Car. D1 GOLD 4X4 and D1 GOLD (API CK-4/SN, P-12 Ester), heavy-duty diesel engine oils (D1 SPORT, D1 PLUS, D1 LONGLIFE, Devo SHPD, Devo ALL FLEET), automatic transmission fluids (ATF HD PRO FLEET to Allison TES 668, ATF HD to Allison TES 389), SUPER-SHIFT manual transmission and hypoid gear oils, power steering, brake and clutch fluids, and HD ELC, HD ELC NOAT and HD antifreeze coolants.
- Powersports: 27 products in eight types. MAXX GOLD 4T (P-10 Ester), MAXX ULTRA 4T and MAXX LAUNCH 4T motorcycle engine oils (JASO MA2), MAXX GOLD and MAXX ULTRA SCOOTER 4T oils (JASO MB), MAXX GOLD 2T and MAXX ULTRA 2T (JASO FD), MAXX-SHIFT gear oils, fork oils, DOT 4 brake fluid, MAXX COOL coolants and MAXX CHAIN LUBE.
- Industrial: spindle bearing, refrigeration, air compressor, turbine, heat transfer, electrical insulating, hydraulic and gear oils.
- Marine: MARINO four-stroke and two-stroke outboard engine oils, marine transmission oils, and cylinder, trunk piston engine, stern tube, auxiliary gear, turbine, hydraulic and compressor oils for ships.
